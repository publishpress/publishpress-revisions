# PublishPress Revisions Support for Yoast SEO (Pro)

This feature requires the Pro version of the PublishPress Revisions plugin.

Yoast SEO is the most popular SEO plugin in WordPress. With the Pro version of [PublishPress Revisions](https://publishpress.com/revisions/) you can submit, moderate, approve and schedule revisions to your WordPress posts, and they will keep all your SEO data.

In the image below, you can Yoast SEO data. With [PublishPress Revisions](https://publishpress.com/links/revisions) Pro you enter this data and it will be saved throughout the revision approval process. Revisions Pro will track your keyphrases, SEO title, meta description, Yoast SEO settings, and more.

If you use Yoast SEO, you will safely be able to make duplicate copies of your content with [PublishPress Revisions](https://publishpress.com/links/revisions) Pro.
