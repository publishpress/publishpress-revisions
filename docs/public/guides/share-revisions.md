# Share Preview of Revisions Before Going Live

Using the [PublishPress Revisions](https://publishpress.com/links/revisions) plugin, you can share a preview of your work before you make it live. The solution in this post is ideal for sharing a preview with people who do not have a login to your site.

- Install the [Public Post Preview plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/public-post-preview/).
- Go to the “[Revision Queue](https://publishpress.com/links/links/revision-queue)” link in your WordPress admin menu.
- Click “Edit”.

![Edit Link Revisions](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-revisions/edit-link-revisions-83b9a925b9.webp)

*Edit Link Revisions*

- Look in the right sidebar of your post.
- Check “Enable public preview”.
- You will now get a preview URL for your revision. You can share this URL with anyone, even if they are not a user on your site.

![Post Preview Url](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-revisions/post-preview-url-50605759fd.webp)

*Post Preview Url*

These preview links will still be valid after you approve the revision. If you visit these links after approval, you will see a red bar saying, “This is a Past Revision”.

![Past Revision](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-revisions/past-revision-9f8458fab2.webp)
