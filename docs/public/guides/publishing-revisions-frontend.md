# How to Manage Revisions on the Frontend

Table of Contents

- [Using the Preview Link for Revisions](#h-using-the-preview-link-for-revisions)
- [Different Colors for the Revisions Toolbar](#1-toc-title)

It is possible to preview and then directly manage revisions via the frontend of your WordPress site.

You can do this with [the PublishPress Revisions plugin](https://publishpress.com/revisionary/) under two conditions:

1. You have sufficient permissions to manage revisions.
2. You can access the “Preview” link for a revision.

## Using the Preview Link for Revisions [#](#h-using-the-preview-link-for-revisions)

- Go to the “[Revision Queue](https://publishpress.com/links/links/revision-queue)” or “[Revision Archive](https://publishpress.com/links/revision-archive)” links in your WordPress admin menu.
- If you have sufficient permissions, you’ll see the “Preview” link.

## Different Colors for the Revisions Toolbar [#](#1-toc-title)

On the frontend of the site, there will be a toolbar across the top of the site. This toolbar will change to reflect the status of the revision.

For example, when looking at a submitted revision, you’ll see this green toolbar. These changes have not yet been approved.

![This is a submitted revisions](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-revisions/submitted-revision-toolbar-ea9f0d9fa4.webp)

*Pubish Links*

The color and options in this toolbar will change based on the status of the revision. For example, this red color is for revisions that are no longer live. In this case, Editors and Administrators can “Restore” the revision.

![Past Revision toolbar](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-revisions/past-revision-toolbar-3545481892.webp)

This purple color shows that you’re looking at a post that’s currently live on the site.

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-revisions/currentrevision-e9c249e363.webp)

A grey bar indicates that you’re looking at a revision that’s scheduled for automatic publication at a later time. Editors and Administrators may choose to publish it ahead of schedule.

![Scheduled revision toolbar](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-revisions/scheduled-revision-toolbar-7244c783e2.webp)

*Scheduled*
