# How to Preview Past Revisions

By default, WordPress doesn’t offer a way to see what old revisions look like. To preview old revisions of your content, install the [PublishPress Revisions](https://publishpress.com/links/revisions) plugin.

When you are editing a post, click the “Revisions” link in the right sidebar:

![Revisions Llink In WordPress](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-revisions/revisions-llink-in-wordpress-e4c2386174.webp)

*Revisions Llink In WordPress*

You are now looking at revisions for a post, and you’ll see a “Preview/Restore” link.

![Preview Restore](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-revisions/preview-restore-a37f0938d5.webp)

*Preview Restore*

This will take you to the front of your site where can see your revision. There will be a bar across the top of the page saying, “This is a Past Revision”. You will have three choices:

1. **Compare**: see how this revision compares to the current version.
2. **View Published Post**: see the current version live on your site.
3. **Restore**: replace the current version with this older revision.

![Past Revision](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-revisions/past-revision-9f8458fab2.webp)

*Past Revision*
